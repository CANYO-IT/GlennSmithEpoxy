Good questions

Before you ask.

Every slab is different, but most residential garage floors land in a predictable range based on size, slab condition, and system. That’s why we do free on-site quotes — you get an exact number, not a guess, and there’s never pressure to book.

Most garages are prepped and coated in a single day, with a return visit for the topcoat when the system calls for it. You can typically walk on the floor in 24 hours and park on it in 3–5 days, depending on the topcoat chemistry and weather.

Peeling is a prep failure, not an epoxy failure. Big-box kits rely on acid etching, which leaves the slab too smooth to bond. We mechanically prep every floor and inspect the slab first — if there’s a moisture concern, we’ll tell you straight before any coating goes down.

Flake systems have a natural orange-peel texture that grips well, and we can add anti-slip aggregate to any topcoat — standard practice for commercial kitchens, pool decks, and entryways. Smooth metallic floors we’ll always spec with a traction additive where water is likely.

A dust mop and an occasional damp mop with a pH-neutral cleaner is the whole routine. No waxing, no resealing for years. Oil, road salt, and household chemicals sit on top of the coating instead of soaking in — they wipe right up.

Almost always, yes. Cracks and spalls get repaired as part of prep, and stains get ground away. If your slab has a serious moisture issue, we’ll tell you straight — with options, not a sales pitch.
